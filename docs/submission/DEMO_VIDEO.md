# Demo video script (about 2 minutes)

Record the screen at 1920x1080 with a voice-over. Host it on YouTube (unlisted is fine) or Google Drive set to "anyone with the link", and test the link in a private window.

If you use a text-to-speech voice for the narration, the Space Apps rules require a note in the video description and metadata that the audio is AI-generated. Your own voice needs no note.

Open `https://globalburnlog.earth/?tour=1` for the guided parts: it starts the tour on the right view and every step is data-driven.

| Time | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:12 | Hero: the night-side Earth with fire glowing across Africa and Asia | "Fire shapes the planet every year. Satellites have recorded it for 25 years, but the record is split across sensors that can't be compared, so patterns are hard to see." |
| 0:12 to 0:30 | Method page: the before/after chart | "The newer VIIRS sensor sees about six times more detections than the older MODIS sensors, so a naive count jumps in 2012. We calibrate the two against each other where they overlap, and carry an uncertainty band on every estimated year." |
| 0:30 to 0:55 | Back to the Explorer, Punjab and Haryana calendar (tour step 1) | "Each row is a year, each column a day. In Punjab and Haryana the same two bands come back every year: April and May, and October and November, the crop-residue burning season." |
| 0:55 to 1:15 | Tour step 2 (climatology) then step 3 (anomaly view) | "In 2025 the autumn burn began around 13 October, about two weeks later than usual, and it was the lowest of the 25 years. The anomaly view shows how unusual every week was against the same week in other years." |
| 1:15 to 1:30 | Tour step 4 (map, November) | "The map shows where it burned that month. Brighter hexagons burned on more days." |
| 1:30 to 1:50 | Search a country (for example Brazil), then pick Southern African savanna in Compare | "This isn't just India. Every country has the same calendar, and you can compare regions: in 2025 southern Africa peaked in late August while Punjab's main peak came in May." |
| 1:50 to 2:05 | Draw a rectangle on the map, then click CSV or Copy link | "You can draw any area on Earth, calibrate it live, and share the exact view or download the data." |
| 2:05 to 2:25 | Method page: the 12-region validation chart | "We checked the seasonal cycle against NASA's burned-area product and against GFED in 12 regions on six continents. Nine match at 0.90 or better. Where a few huge fires dominate, the match is weaker, and the site says so." |
| 2:25 to 2:35 | About page, then the globalburnlog.earth address | "Built on NASA FIRMS data, open and reproducible. Global Burn Log: globalburnlog.earth." |

## Tips
- Use the 1440 or 1920 wide browser window and zoom to 100%.
- Pause 1 to 2 seconds on each chart so viewers can read it.
- Do one dry run first: the guided tour moves the page for you, so you can talk while it scrolls.
- Keep the final video public and login-free. Do not upload the video to the Space Apps page itself (images only); paste the link.
