# Demo slides (7 slides, as the Space Apps project form requires)

The project form accepts a demo of **no more than 7 slides, including the title and introduction**, or a video of 30 seconds or less. The deck is
`docs/Global_Burn_Log_Demo_Slides.pptx` (editable) and `docs/Global_Burn_Log_Demo_Slides.pdf` (the file to link on the form).

1. **Title and introduction.** Global Burn Log, team QuickSort, globalburnlog.earth.
2. **The problem.** The 2012 jump is the instrument, not the world: raw India record 6.5 times higher after VIIRS starts, 1.13 times after harmonising.
3. **Our approach.** Four steps: count cell-days, make VIIRS MODIS-equivalent, calibrate across sensors, show the uncertainty. Built on NASA FIRMS.
4. **Live example.** Punjab and Haryana: autumn season typically starts 3 Oct, peaks the week of 26 Oct, 2025 the quietest of 25 years.
5. **Unusual conditions and early warning.** The "Unusual" map (Canada 2023), latest week, phone alerts, furthest-from-normal board, season countdown.
6. **Evidence.** India vs NASA MCD64A1 r = 0.93; GFED average 0.84; world annual total predicted within a median 1.6% on years left out; what was rejected; where it is weak.
7. **Impact and close.** Responders, land managers, scientists; open by design; links; AI disclosure.

Every slide has speaker notes. The longer 14-slide outline inside `docs/Global_Burn_Log_Project_Draft.pdf` is for a longer talk, not for the form.
