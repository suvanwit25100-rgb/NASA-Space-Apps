Global Burn Log. How it works

From NASA satellite detections to a calendar in your browser, with no server to keep alive.

1 · NASA data

NASAFIRMS archivePublic per-country annual files, 209 countries, 2000 to 2025MODIS Terra and Aqua (1 km), VIIRS Suomi NPP (375 m)
NASAMCD64A1 burned areaMODIS burned-area product via Microsoft Planetary ComputerUsed only to validate
GFED4.1sBurned area and emissionsSecond validation, 12 regions
→

2 · Python pipeline

FilterVegetation fire only (drops volcanoes, flares, offshore); drops low-confidence detections
GridRaw points become H3 burning cell-days; raw points are discarded (100 M cell-days kept)
CalibrateSeasonal Aqua↔VIIRS and Terra↔Aqua ratios per area put 2000 to 2011 on the VIIRS scale, with an uncertainty band
Detect seasonsFire seasons, peaks and anomalies computed per area and year
→

3 · Static files

series/*.json.gzCalibrated daily series for 209 countries, the world and 16 regions (5 MB total)
map/*.bin.gzYearly global hexagon layers (7 MB for 26 years)
tiles/*.bin.gz5° cell-day tiles so any drawn area can be calibrated in the browser
Dailynrt.json + alertsA GitHub Action reads the newest NASA near-real-time week, writes nrt.json and pushes alerts through ntfy.sh
Firebase Hosting (CDN)globalburnlog.earth, HTTPS, always on, no login
→

4 · In the browser

Calendar heatmapYear × day, hover for value, anomaly and confidence

Verdict and latest weekIs this year unusual, is this week unusual, watchlist and phone alerts
Custom areasDraw, upload GeoJSON, compare, export CSV, PNG or a one-page report
Validation (independent of the pipeline)Seasonal cycle vs NASA MCD64A1: r = 0.93 (India). Vs GFED burned area in 12 regions: 7 of 12 at r ≥ 0.90, average 0.84. Hold-out test, one year left out at a time: the world's annual total is predicted within a median 2%. Weakest where a few huge fires dominate (California, Boreal Canada, Southeast Australia); stated on the site.
ReproducibleEvery step is a script in the repository. The historical archive needs no API key; the in-browser calibration is a line-for-line port of the Python and was checked to give identical results.
